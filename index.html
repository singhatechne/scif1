SCIF Nepal · Kirtipur-06, Chobhar, Kathmandu

A Home, an Education and a Future for Every Child

Supporting Children Initiative Foundation (SCIF) is a small non-profit children's home in Kathmandu, giving 15 children aged 5 to 18 shelter, food, schooling, love and care.

15children, one family

About SCIF

A small home with a very big heart

SCIF began with just four children and a house manager's sister. Over the years the family grew to the 15 children we care for today, looked after by a house manager and two dedicated staff who take minimal wages to cook, clean and tutor.

Our dream is to put every child through university, so they can find a decent job and one day become a beacon of light for less fortunate children.

  • Tutor & help with homework
  • Stationery & school supplies
  • Rice, oil, fruits & food
  • Warm clothes for winter

University is the dream

Our goal is to put every child through university so they can find a decent job and become a beacon of light for less fortunate children.

Volunteers from around the world

Visitors from Europe, the USA and beyond stay from one day to several months to tutor, cook and share new skills.

Community funded

The children's expenses are funded by a changing group of friends, a core supporter and our volunteer program. Every dollar helps on food and house expenses.

Our Approach

Love, routine and opportunity: the things we give every child under our roof.

Shelter, Food & Clothing

A safe, warm home with nutritious meals and clothes for all four seasons, because our house is not heated and winters are hard.

Learn more →

Quality Education

School admission, uniforms, books and homework help every evening, so each child can reach their full potential.

Learn more →

Health & Wellbeing

Regular eye screening, dental camps and hygiene education, run with partners such as People's Dental College and Rotaract.

Learn more →

Volunteer Exchange

Volunteers tutor, cook, teach new skills and share their culture, giving the children a wider view of the world.

Learn more →

Culture & Joyful Childhood

Festivals, hikes, picnics, football and talent shows. Dolma Tamang, for one, won 2nd place in a school singing contest.

A Self-Sustaining Future

Linking with Eyes On Nepal, a local tour operator, to create jobs so graduates can support themselves and carry the legacy forward.
